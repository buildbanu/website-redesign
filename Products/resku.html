The application

Structural firefighting.
Rescue operations.
Complex interiors.

Resku addresses the spatial and communication blind spots of challenging rescue environments. Multiple sensor inputs help build a more complete picture than any single input can provide.

01Spatial mapping
LiDAR-based SLAM builds point-cloud models of structural interiors.
02Thermal context
Thermal sensing adds another perspective on conditions within the environment.
03Responder telemetry
Responder and environmental information become part of the connected system.
