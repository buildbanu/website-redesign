Resku combines LiDAR spatial mapping, thermal fusion, and responder monitoring to address the blind spots of challenging rescue environments.

Onboard computing brings these inputs together close to the point of sensing.

01 / Map

LiDAR spatial SLAM

Build 3D point-cloud models of interiors using onboard spatial mapping.

02 / Sense

Thermal fusion

Combine spatial information with thermal sensing for another perspective on the environment.

03 / Connect

Responder telemetry

Bring responder and environmental information into a connected awareness system.
