01 / Sensing

Different inputs.
One environment.

LiDAR point clouds describe spatial structure. Long-wave infrared arrays contribute thermal information. Sensor fusion brings these complementary inputs into a common architecture.

This work focuses on understanding complex interiors and conditions that limit ordinary visibility.

LiDAR SLAM · Thermal sensing · Sensor fusion
02 / Computing

Keep the work
on board.

Custom hardware and FPGA-accelerated processing bring inference onto the sensor node. Processing close to the source reduces reliance on a remote connection.

The architecture is designed around environments where cloud and GPS connectivity may be unavailable.

Edge computing · Onboard inference
03 / Networking

A picture the team
can share.

Peer-to-peer mesh networking connects spatial information and responder telemetry across the team.

The objective is a shared understanding of the operation: the environment, the conditions, and the people moving through it.

Mesh networking · Responder telemetry
